ANALOGYA staffing rule, and where it breaks. Think of the HPA as a standing rule for a support team: keep everyone about 60% busy, with never fewer than three people on shift and never more than ten. Each person called in needs a desk, as each new Pod needs room on a node. When the office is full, someone has to rent more space, which takes far longer than calling a person in, and that slower job is the cluster autoscaler’s.
